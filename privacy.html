Privacy Policy – Language Guru

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Language Guru is a language-learning application designed to provide lessons, games, practice activities, progress features and AI-assisted learning.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Language Guru may process information such as:

  • Information you provide when using account or sign-in features, if enabled.
  • Learning progress, lesson activity, scores, achievements and preferences.
  • Information you enter into AI learning features, such as questions, answers or practice text, when you choose to use those features.
  • Basic app, device and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure and improve the app.
  • Advertising-related information used to display and measure advertisements, where advertising is enabled.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate Language Guru and its learning features.
  • To save learning progress and preferences.
  • To provide AI-assisted learning responses when you use AI features.
  • To maintain app security, troubleshoot problems and improve performance.
  • To display and measure advertisements when ads are enabled.

3. Advertising

Language Guru may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may process device and advertising-related information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings. You can review Google's information about advertising and privacy through Google's official resources.

4. AI Features

If you use an AI-assisted feature, the text or information you submit may be processed by the AI service used by the app to generate a response. Please do not submit passwords, payment information or other highly sensitive information to AI features.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for functions such as analytics, authentication, cloud services, AI processing, crash/diagnostic support and advertising. These services process information according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Where an account or stored user data can be deleted through the app, users may use the available deletion controls. You may also contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing to request assistance with privacy or data-deletion questions.

8. Children's Privacy

Language Guru may be used for language learning by children and families. We aim to minimize the collection of personal information. Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's information or wish to request deletion may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing.

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to use optional features that require additional information, and you can control certain advertising preferences through your Android device and Google account settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, please use the developer contact information shown on the Language Guru Google Play listing.
